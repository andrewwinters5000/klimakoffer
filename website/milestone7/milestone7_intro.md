# Milestone 7 - Introduction

This is the "mysterious" milestone 7.
From the baseline Klimakoffer implementation from milestones 1-6, the goal of this milestone is for each group to select an modification, extension, or even a completely different strategy to the EBM model.
Some extensions of the Klimakoffer capabilities are available as bonus exercises in milestones 1-3.
Many of the topics for this milestone give focus to uncertainty quantification (UQ) and other mathematical tools that aim to model and measure errors present in the simulation.

Errors are unavoidable in climate modeling, as well as many other scientific fields that deal with such nonlinear processes.
In this and other courses we may have seen how these errors manifest.
They come from a variety of sources.
Some examples are:

1. the finite precision of the computer.
2. the resolution in space and time of the discretization.
3. assuming a constant albedo value for land. In reality, the heat capacity of a desert versus is dense forest is very different.
4. measurement errors in data that are used in the tuning of empirical modeling coefficients.
